# Parity Proof, one page

**Problem.** Changes ship that nobody can fully check: AI-agent pull requests merged because tests passed, legacy apps rebuilt without a spec. The bugs that slip through are business bugs: an invoice total off by one cent while every line looks right.

**Customer.** First: developers in teams using AI coding agents, freelancers and IT consultants doing migrations (panel try-rates 43 % to 50 %). Later: owners who must sign off a rebuilt app, through their provider.

**Offer and price.** Record your app once; Parity replays it before and after any change and shows every field that differs, with evidence. The comparison is plain code. Nothing leaves the machine unless you send it. Proven scenarios export as Playwright tests. Guarantee: every field matches, or every difference is shown with its evidence. **Free and open source.** A hosted tier ($19 to $29 per app per month) and a one-off migration report ($490 to $990) are scenarios to test after the hackathon.

| number (hosted-tier scenario) | value |
|---|---|
| Contribution per app-month | $22.66 (78 %) |
| Break-even | 3 paying apps a month |
| Year 1 operating profit | $3,527 (founder paid $0) |
| Cash needed | $108 |
| Hackathon budget | $150 Meta credits, about 23 full runs |
| Verdict (`compile.py`) | Profitable, for the scenario only |

**Evidence.**
- Buyer panel, 20 simulated buyers: 15 % would try pitch v1 and 40 % would try the offer. These are trials, not payments.
- Competitor gap: no tool proves that any app (old Windows, legacy web) behaves the same end to end in a form a non-developer can read. The equivalence players are mainframe-only, and the open tools stop at the API.
- Real test: not run yet (one-cent post, Oct 27).

**What the money is for.** No money is needed. The ask is time and attention: try it on your app after Nov 4, and vote for it.

_The panel is simulated buyers and the numbers are projections; real users and real quotes confirm them. Not financial, legal or tax advice._
