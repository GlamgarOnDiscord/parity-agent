# Offers lens
Score: 6/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The dream outcome is big and easy to grasp: "the new version does exactly what the old one did, field by field, with evidence." Owners, auditors and anyone reviewing an AI-written change want that certainty.
- Time to a first result is short: record a video, get rules and a replayable suite. That is a low-effort way in compared with writing tests by hand.
- Deterministic replay with no AI at check time makes the result believable and cheap to run again. That is the start of a guarantee.
- There is one real piece of proof already: the one-cent tax-on-tax rounding difference in Virtual Salesman 3. That is exactly the kind of "you would never have caught this" moment that makes people feel stupid saying no.
- The judging fits well: open weights, SAM, Muse Voice, Spark and Code all doing real work covers the 25 % for Meta tooling without forcing it.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Likelihood is zero today.** Nothing is built, and the pipeline has five stages with computer-use exploration under Wine, all on one laptop short on RAM in 9 days. If the demo cannot show a real difference caught end to end, the main thing the offer depends on (believing it works) falls apart, for judges and users alike.
2. **"As many people as possible" is not a market.** This lens picks the market first. Developers using AI agents, IT service providers and non-technical owners differ in pain, budget, reach and effort tolerance. A pitch spread across all three weakens the 30 % for impact, because no specific person's loss is being prevented.
3. **The effort falls on the buyer.** A human expert has to validate every rule, and each app needs its own sandbox. For developers, the alternative is the test suite they already have; for owners, the expert they would need is often the person who retired. If validation feels like work, adoption stalls.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Who exactly loses money when parity breaks, and how much? (No number exists; open question.)
- What is the smallest demo that shows a real, costly difference caught, explained and fixed in under 3 minutes?
- How many minutes of expert validation does one app need? That cost decides adoption.
- For developers with tests already, what does this catch that their suite misses?
- What would someone pay later, and who pays: the service provider or the owner? (Unknown; open question.)
- What would a guarantee look like: "every field matches, or every difference is shown with evidence"?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Pitch one buyer: the IT service provider rebuilding a legacy business app. Keep "any change to any software" for one closing line.
2. The demo catches the one-cent rounding bug live, end to end, on Virtual Salesman 3, with the evidence on screen.
3. Cut or stub autonomous exploration if it puts the core loop at risk (record, extract rules, validate, replay, diff).
4. State the guarantee in one sentence and give it a clear name.
5. Show expert validation as a few clicks, timed on screen.
