Parity: proof that your software still does the same thing after a change.
Record your app in use once (a screen recording, optionally narrated). Parity explores the rest on its own in a sandbox and writes the business rules it found in plain language for you to confirm.
After any change (a refactor by an AI coding agent, a framework upgrade, a full rewrite of an old app), Parity replays the same scenarios on the old and new versions and shows every field that differs, with the evidence.
Works on web apps and old Windows apps. Uses Meta's AI models to capture and explain; the replays themselves run without AI.
Free and open source on GitHub. Runs on your machine.
